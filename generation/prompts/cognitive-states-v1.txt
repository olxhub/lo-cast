Use case: identity-preserve

Create a 4-column by 3-row sheet of the same person from Image 1 in these
twelve learning states, left to right:

Row 1: listening; reading a book; studying; writing.
Row 2: checking work; calculating with a physical calculator resting on an
implied surface immediately below the frame; thinking with a hand at the chin
and gaze upward; processing with a hand at the chin and gaze downward.
Row 3: confused while scratching the side or top of the head; uncertain;
realizing something with a raised index finger; understanding.

Let facial expression, gaze, body language, hands, and natural props
communicate each state. The calculator's upper portion and the hand pressing
its keys may enter obliquely from the bottom edge.

Keep identity, apparent age, hair, clothing, warm painterly style, beige
background, existing close bust framing, face scale, and camera position
consistent. Keep complete heads, hair, headwear, and meaning-bearing fingers
visible. Use clear pale separator gutters.
