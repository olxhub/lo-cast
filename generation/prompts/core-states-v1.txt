Use case: identity-preserve
Asset type: 24-expression avatar sheet for scenario-based learning

Image 1 is the authoritative identity, age, hairstyle, skin tone, clothing,
painterly finish, and warm beige background reference. Create exactly one
4-column by 6-row contact sheet showing this same person in 24 distinct
expressions. All 24 panels must be occupied.

Panel order, left to right:
Row 1: blank; calm; explaining while speaking; concerned.
Row 2: concerned fear; contempt; driven/determined; fear.
Row 3: serious; suspicious side-eye; warm smile; very angry.
Row 4: laughing openly; crying with visible tears; facepalm with one hand;
       lecturing with one index finger raised.
Row 5: awe; hectic/overwhelmed with hands near head; rage; eyes peacefully
       closed.
Row 6: cheeky/sly; pleading; embarrassed; loving broad grin.

Identity lock: preserve the exact same recognizable face, age, hair, skin,
body proportions, and clothing in every panel. No identity drift and no
hairstyle or wardrobe changes.

Style: match the supplied semi-realistic warm painterly editorial portrait,
natural anatomy and skin texture, fine hatched brushwork, and warm beige
mottled background.

Composition: exactly 24 equal square crop-ready portrait panels separated by
continuous pale cream internal and outer gutters. Use medium bust framing,
front-facing or only slight natural turns.

Critical safe area: the complete head and all hair must be visible in every
panel, with generous empty background above the crown (about 12% of panel
height). No hair, crown, forehead, chin, shoulders, raised finger, or expressive
hand may touch or cross a panel edge. Frame slightly wider than seems
necessary.

Constraints: one person in every panel; no empty cells; no labels, names,
captions, text, logos, watermark, duplicated faces, panel bleed, or merged
cells.

For a retry after an incorrect row count, reinforce:
"Create a tall portrait contact-sheet canvas containing exactly four columns
and six rows. Six horizontal rows are mandatory. Count them before finishing."
